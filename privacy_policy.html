Privacy Policy for Dweep Diary Mobile App

Effective Date: November 2026

Website: https://dweepdiary.in

1. Overview

Dweep Diary ("we", "our", or "us") provides this news application as a free, informative service delivering news, stories, and updates from the Lakshadweep islands and surrounding regions.

2. Information Collection and Use

Our app does not require user registration or personal login to read news articles. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, home address, or phone number.

3. Bookmarks and Local Storage

Articles saved for offline reading are stored locally on your device via shared storage. We do not transmit or store your bookmarks on our servers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or IDFA) to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Push Notifications

We may use push notification services (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging or OneSignal) to send you real-time breaking news alerts. You can disable notifications at any time from your device system settings.

6. External Links

Articles may contain links to external websites, including our official portal dweepdiary.in. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party sites.
